How to use Draggable Video Pop Out

Hover any video, pop it out, and keep watching while you scroll.

1. Hover any video

Buttons appear in the video's top corner. They fade when your mouse rests.

Pick a side in the extension menu.

  • Pop out into a floating window, right where the video was
  • Picture in picture, Chrome's always-on-top window
  • Download, on regular video files only

2. Drag and resize

Drag to move, pull any edge to resize, close to put it back. Hold Shift to change the shape.

Other ways to pop out

Alt+Shift+V or Option+Shift+V on Mac

Pin it for quick settings

Extensions
Draggable Video Pop Out: Floating Picture in Picture Player

Click the puzzle icon by your address bar, then the pin.

Good to know
  • If the buttons don't show on a tab that was already open, refresh it.
  • Download isn't available on YouTube or on sites that stream video in pieces, which includes most videos on X, Reddit and Twitch.
  • Some feeds delete posts you've scrolled far past. When that happens, the floating window closes with it.
