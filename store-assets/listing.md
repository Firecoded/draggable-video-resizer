# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Draggable Video Pop Out: Floating Picture in Picture Player

## Summary
Pop out any video into a floating, resizable player that stays put while you scroll, with picture in picture in one click.

## Description
The video popout that does more than picture in picture.

Hover any video and pop it out in one click, with no need to press play first. Drag the floating player anywhere and resize it from any edge, up to the size of your browser window. Prefer an always-on-top window? Picture in picture is one click away too.

WHAT IT DOES
- Pop out: float any video above the page, at nearly its original size
- Picture in picture: send it to Chrome's always-on-top window, in one click
- Embedded players: pop out videos embedded in articles and blogs without reloading them
- Built-in controls: play, volume, seek, speed, loop and fullscreen
- Download: save videos that are regular files (optional, asks permission first)
- Keyboard shortcut and right-click menu
- Light, dark and system themes
- Turn the buttons off on any site

WORKS ON
YouTube, X, Vimeo, Twitch and most other sites with video.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Download isn't available on YouTube or on sites that stream video in pieces, which includes most videos on X, Reddit and Twitch.
- Some feeds delete posts you've scrolled far past. If that happens, the floating window closes with it.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no tracking and no data collected. Your settings are stored by Chrome.

## Privacy tab
- Single purpose: Lets users pop videos out of a page into a floating, resizable window or picture in picture.
- Access to all sites: Needed to find videos and show the toolbar on whatever page the user is viewing. No page data is collected or sent anywhere.
- activeTab: Reads the current site's name for "Show on this site" and sends "Pop out all videos" to the current tab.
- storage: Saves the user's theme and toolbar settings.
- contextMenus: Adds "Pop out video" to the right-click menu.
- downloads (optional): Saves a video when the user clicks the download button. Only requested on first use.
